Who we are

Viewers

This website does not use cookies, does not run analytics and does not collect personal information. When you watch our videos on YouTube, YouTube's own terms and privacy policy apply.

Our use of YouTube API Services

NowThen uses YouTube API Services only to upload and schedule videos on its own YouTube channel. The application acts solely on the NowThen channel's account. It does not request, collect, store or share data from any other YouTube user or Google account.

The access granted to the application is kept as an encrypted secret in our publishing system and is used only to upload our own videos. You can revoke any application's access to a Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

By watching or interacting with our content you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
